How lead partnerships work

  1. A visitor completes a four-step request: need, vehicle, details, contact.
  2. They give express consent to be contacted by matched partners, recorded with the time and page.
  3. We route the request to partners who serve that vertical and area, by email or webhook.
  4. You contact the consumer and quote. Pricing per request is agreed up front, exclusive or shared.

We do not sell requests to unrelated marketers, we never collect Social Security numbers or card details, and we remove anyone who asks to opt out.

Our advertising standards

  • Sponsored placements are always labelled. Sponsors never edit or approve our guides or calculator results.
  • We don't accept advertising for title loans, predatory lending, or products we believe mislead drivers.
  • Display ads are served by Google AdSense under Google's publisher policies.
  • Partners must be properly licensed where their service requires it.
